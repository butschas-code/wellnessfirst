The rulebook

How a birth chart becomes every personal program: three a day (morning, midday, evening), one for the current chapter of life, two that build on strengths and two that give gentle support. Nothing is picked from the catalog. Every rate is worked out from the chart, so no two people get the same program, and the day programs are new each day. No AI is involved: the same chart on the same day always gives the same programs.

How a day program is built

Each program is an arc: a few movements that follow the time of day. Each movement has its own rates, its own length, a slow drift around the rate, and sometimes short bursts or a breathing swell. The chart decides every one of these.

1. Read the chart in depthSix-fold strength of every planet, birth degrees, the Moon's station and nadi, element balance, ascendant, the three running periods, and today's Moon, weekday and hour rulers.
2. Find your tonesEvery planet gets a tone from its orbit, turned by the degree it stood at at your birth. Your home tone is the blend of all nine, weighted by their strength.
3. Shape the arcThe time of day sets the movements. Your constitution, your strengths and challenges, and today's pace add, lengthen, soften or skip movements.
4. Play and holdThe arc plays as a pulse in the music. It glides between rates, drifts gently, and then holds its last movement until you change it.

Your tones

The method is the cosmic octave: a planet's orbit is a very slow rhythm, and doubling it again and again (raising it by octaves) brings it into hearing range. The orbit gives each planet its place in the octave. Two things then make it personal:

Your birth degree. One full turn of the zodiac counts as one octave, Kepler's old mapping. Each planet's tone is turned by the degree it stood at when you were born, so Mars in your chart sounds different from Mars in anyone else's.

Your strength. When several planets voice a movement, their tones are blended and each one counts by its six-fold strength (shadbala). Strong planets pull the blend towards themselves. The blend is then placed inside the movement's band: 12–15 Hz for a lift, 4.5–6.5 Hz for a release, and so on.

Your home tone

This is the blend of all nine planets, with the Moon and the ascendant lord counted extra, placed between 8.5 and 11.5 Hz. It plays the part of an individual alpha rhythm (research tunes stimulation to each person's own resting rhythm, which varies between people). Every program passes through it.

Your constitution

An Ayurvedic-style lean towards airy (vata), fiery (pitta) or earthy (kapha). It comes from the Moon's nadi (counts double), the element balance of the chart, and the ascendant's element. It changes the shape of the programs, never the planets' tones.

Chapter, strengths and gentle support

These four kinds of program are not tied to a time of day. Each is a theme arc of five movements, about 22 minutes, that holds its last movement:

Arrive
home tone · 3 min

Starts from your own resting rate, with a breathing swell.

Move in
home → theme band · 4 min

Glides into the band of the theme planet. Its tone is turned by its birth degree and blended with the ascendant lord (strengths), the Moon (support) or the sub-period lord (chapter).

Stay
theme band · 6 min

The heart of the program. Support arcs may use a research value here: 10 Hz renewal, 7.83 Hz grounding, or 40 Hz bursts for focus.

Deepen
brighter half (strength), softer half (support) · 5 min

Strengths lean further in; support eases further down. The chapter deepens into its sub-period.

Back to centre
halfway to home · 4 min, then holds

Settles halfway between the theme and your home tone, and stays.

Strengths are your two strongest planets that are good or strong. Support goes first to a low 12th, 4th or 6th area (only with a birth time). After that it goes to low planets, then to the weakest average ones, and never to a planet that is also a strength. The chapter follows the main period lord. It uses the gentle version when the period has low backing or Saturn is pressing on the Moon. Strengths and support never change from day to day. The chapter changes when the sub-period does.

Research values

Drift
From the Infinity jitter spec: ±5 % for calm and sleep, ±10 % for activation, so the pattern never sits still (against habituation). The device updates every 0.3–3 s; the audio glides every 2–6 s, which sounds smoother in music.
0.1 Hz breathing
About six breaths a minute (range 4.5–6.5) is the resonance rate of heart-rate variability (Lehrer & Gevirtz 2014). Evenings slow to 5 and 4.5 a minute.
7.83 Hz
Schumann resonance. It is used for grounding throughout the device catalog (Deep Relaxation, Meditation Support, Emotional Grounding).
40 Hz bursts
The auditory steady-state response is strongest near 40 Hz (review). The pattern, 2 s every 15 s, is the device catalog's Focus Boost.
10 Hz renewal
The rate most studied for cell-level renewal in pulsed-field research (Goodwin, NASA TP-2003-212054). It is used in the device's recovery programs.
≤ 1 Hz ending
Pulsed-field sleep trials use slow rates (Pelka et al. 2001), and they match the slow oscillation of deep sleep.
Arc shapes
These follow the device catalog's protocols. Morning Smooth Wake-Up and Morning Activation run 5 → 10 → 15–18 Hz, Focus Boost and Productivity Mode run 10–16 Hz, and Evening Wind-Down and Deep Sleep Induction run 12 → 8 → 5 → 4 Hz. Bands follow the whitepaper: delta 2–4, theta 4–8, alpha 8–12, low beta 12–20 Hz.
Your own rate
Stimulation tuned to a person's own alpha rhythm is an active research line, with mixed results (review). Here the chart stands in for an EEG measurement. That is our design choice, not a research finding.

The Pro view shows every rate, its voices with their weights, the rules that fired, and, when a rate lands within 1 % of a value in the 105-program catalog, which program holds it. That link is shown for reference only; it never decides anything.

Safety limits

Checked every time a program is loaded. A program outside them is refused, not played. Rates stay between 0.5 and 40 Hz (pulse only: no tones, no binaural). Each movement runs 1 to 15 minutes, and a whole program at most 45. Pulse depth is at most 0.4, drift at most ±12 %, and the breathing swell 3 to 7 a minute. Bursts last at most 4 s and come at least 8 s apart. Visuals never flash faster than once a second.

What needs your decision

  • The tone method. Is orbit tones turned by birth degree, blended by strength, right for Wellness First? The alternative is to drop the birth-degree turn and use the plain cosmic-octave tones. That is more traditional, but people's programs then sound much more alike.
  • The voices of each movement: the Moon at waking, Mercury for focus, Saturn and Ketu for release, Venus for the pause.
  • The constitution rules, and that the Moon's nadi counts double.
  • Hour rulers from 06:00 in equal hours. Real sunrise would be more exact but needs the member's current location, which we do not ask for.
  • Lengths and peaks. Mornings peak at up to 22 Hz, middays at 19, and evenings end near 1 Hz and hold there.
  • The theme bands and titles. Each planet has a band and a title for building on it and for supporting it (the table above). Saturn sits low (4.5–6.5 Hz) both ways; Rahu's support is grounding at 7.83 Hz.
  • Support picks. Low life areas come before low planets, and the second place can go to an average planet.
